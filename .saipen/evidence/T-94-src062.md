  None of this exists on a Linux container.

## Manual test steps (Windows, on a build carrying `50e0cf6` and `811bfb5`)

### 1. ProTrail everywhere
1. Settings → ProTrail → General. "ProTrail is on" ticked, "Everywhere in
   Windows" selected. Within ~2 s the status reads "Drawing over N monitors,
   reading moves and clicks (Raw Input)". The first start compiles the
   helper once, so allow a moment.
2. Move the mouse over the desktop, Explorer and a browser. A yellow trail
   follows the cursor on every monitor, including one with another scaling.
3. Click in another app (open a folder, click a link). A ring appears and
   the click still reaches that app. Nothing is ever blocked.
4. Hold the left button for about a second, then release: a charging aura,
   then a bigger ring. Drag while holding: the motion wake.
5. Alt+Tab and the taskbar show no ProTrail windows. ZAICODE's hotkey
   toggle still targets the ZAICODE window.
6. Pick "Only inside ZAICODE": the trail shows only over the ZAICODE window.
   Switch back to Everywhere.
7. Trail tab: try Neon + Glitter, and Comet with Pixel size 3. Click tab:
   try Fire, then Water. Every change applies at once.
8. Close ZAICODE to the tray: the trail keeps drawing. Exit ZAICODE fully:
   the trail is gone. With several monitors, unplug or replug one while it
   runs: the overlays follow.
9. If the status says "clicks are not seen", report its text. It names why
   the helper did not start, and the trail still follows the cursor.

### 2. Sidebar footer
1. Footer: Problip, then the ProTrail pointer, then the gear. Left-click
   ProTrail: it turns grey and the trail stops. Click again: it comes back.
2. Right-click ProTrail: Settings → ProTrail opens.
3. Right-click an empty part of the footer row: the Footer editor. Pick
   "Avatar only". The name disappears (hover the avatar for it) and the
   buttons take the width. Tick Timers, Help and Theme, then drag to
   reorder. Anything that does not fit goes into ⋯.
4. Settings → Sidebar → Footer shows the same list. Defaults restores it.

### 3. SAIPEGGLE
1. Settings, left column: "SAIPEGGLE" sits right above "Support Developer",
   with a gear on the same row. The gear opens the game on its Settings tab.
   The SAIPEGGLE row opens the game on Play.
2. The game covers the whole window. Level 1-1 intro: click. Aim with the
   mouse: the dotted guide shows the path up to the first peg. Click to
   fire. Peg hits sound one note higher each time. Lit pegs pop after the
   ball is gone.
3. Land a ball in the moving bucket: "FREE BALL" and the ball count grows.
   Hit a green peg: Super Guide, and the next shots show the whole path.
4. Clear every orange peg: "EXTREME FEVER", slow motion, 8-bit Ode to Joy,
   then the 10K / 50K / 100K buckets. "LEVEL CLEAR", click: 1-2 opens.
5. Levels tab: 11 stages × 5, 1-2 now open, best scores shown. Quick play:
   New seed, then Play this seed. Copy the board code, paste it, play it.
6. P pauses. Esc pauses, and Esc again opens the Levels menu. Esc there, or
   Exit, returns to ZAICODE. Typing PEGGLE outside a text field opens the
   game.
7. Settings tab: change Balls, Gravity, Colours → Classic pegs. The level
   restarts with the change. Switch the ZAICODE palette, reopen the game:
   board and pegs follow the palette.
8. Settings → Sounds → SAIPEGGLE: every cue has its own row. Mute "Peg
   hit": the hits go quiet in the game.
9. At 125% / 150% Windows scaling every game pixel is sharp: no blur, no
   uneven pixels.

### 4. Session text
Settings → Session text: pick the "Word document" preset. An agent answer
shows blue numbered headings. Pick "Terminal": monospace. Change one part
(Heading 1 colour), save it as a preset, export it, import it back.

### 5. DONE mark
A project whose SAIPEN board has no open ticket shows the DONE mark on its
sidebar row, with an offer to start an A3 audit wave.

### 6. Model picker
Composer model menu: every subscription account shows a visible readiness
bar in its palette colours. Colours do not mix between rows.

### 7. Change numbers in the chat
1. Let an agent edit a file. Its Edit row in the chat plays -N first, then
   +N. Old chats stay quiet.
2. Settings → Highlights & motion → Change numbers in the chat: change the
   timings and try the demo rows.
3. Turn on the calm interface with "play under the calm interface" ticked:
   the numbers still play. The preview digits never overlap.

### 8. Sounds and navigation
With and without animations, click between projects in the sidebar: one
sound per navigation, no late second sound, no pile-up.

### 9. Project click and ◆
1. Click a project with a MAIN session: MAIN opens. Click it again: the row
   folds and unfolds, and never opens a random session.
2. Click ◆ on a session row: that session becomes the project's MAIN and
   the others become its children.

### 10. Splash
Start ZAICODE, then use a custom splash picture. The first splash shows the
SAIPEN emblem until the picture is ready, never a cut-off fragment.
Rebuild the launcher (`tools\launcher\build.cmd`) and start through it at
125% scaling: the splash is not cropped.
